This survey reviews results on the Hausdorff dimension of shrinking target sets and recurrence sets in dynamical systems. We analyze exponential mixing for analytic observables and show that mixing at a double exponential rate occurs for genuinely nonlinear systems. Neither its presence nor its sharp exponent is stable under arbitrarily small area-preserving perturbations in the real-analytic topology. We construct two families of area-preserving Anosov Blaschke product diffeomorphisms that converge in the real-analytic topology to the same hyperbolic toral automorphism. For nonzero parameters, both deformations are topologically conjugate to the linear model but not -conjugate. Original abstract

Optimal Morse vector fields on closed surfaces, i.e. fields with the minimal possible number of critical points (a single source and a single sink), are classified up to topological trajectory equivalence by one-cycle signed chord diagrams in Kybalko, Prishlyak and Shchurko (2018). We recall this construction and the associated duality, which corresponds to reversing the direction of the flow, in a unified encoding in which both endpoints of a chord carry the same label, and we make it algorithmic. We give an explicit traversal procedure that detects one-cycle diagrams, a canonical form of an equivalence class under cyclic shifts, reversal and relabeling of chords, and a construction of the dual diagram. On this basis we describe an algorithm that enumerates all one-cycle signed chord diagrams with a given number of chords, both for orientable and for non-orientable surfaces, groups them into equivalence classes, pairs each class with its dual, and identifies the self-dual ones. The algorithm is implemented in Python and archived in Zenodo; its output is validated against the Harer–Zagier numbers in the orientable case and against orbit-size counts in both cases. We also note that, when a one-cycle diagram is read as a cellular embedding of a one-vertex graph, its dual diagram describes the embedding of the dual graph.

Original abstract

We study the structure of the centralizer of a non-elliptic holomorphic self-map of the unit disc and its relation with continuous dynamical structures. We prove that if the identity is not isolated in the centralizer of a non-elliptic self-map , then is univalent and its centralizer contains a non-trivial continuous one-parameter semigroup of holomorphic self-maps. This result provides a general criterion for the existence of continuous dynamics arising from discrete iteration. Our approach is based on the theory of holomorphic models and on simultaneous linearization techniques for commuting self-maps. In particular, we exploit recent developments on simultaneous linearization for parabolic self-maps with positive hyperbolic step. As a consequence, we obtain new sufficient conditions for the embeddability of holomorphic self-maps into continuous semigroups.
