Original abstract

We study N particles moving in a dispersing billiard under an electric field and coupled through a Gaussian thermostat. The corresponding mean-field equation is a Vlasov-type equation with specular reflection. We reformulate the equation as a fixed point problem for the time-integrated thermostat coefficient.
