Original abstract

We introduce two complementary frameworks for studying local periodicity and recurrence in dynamical systems. In the topological setting, we consider continuous maps with nonempty periodic-point sets, restricted to the closures of their periodic points. We define the periodic penetration time of an open set as the least period of a periodic point it contains. Using dynamically refined finite generating covers, we construct asymptotic mean and variance invariants that describe local periodic penetration relative to the logarithms of minimum subcover cardinalities. We establish their invariance under topological conjugacy and investigate their behavior for finite periodic systems, full shifts, shifts of finite type, and zero-entropy systems with dense periodic points. We also extend the construction to invertible systems using symmetric dynamical refinements. In the measure theoretic setting, we define local return times through positive measure intersections of measurable sets with their iterated preimages. Using dynamically refined finite generating partitions, we construct corresponding asymptotic mean and variance invariants and establish their invariance under measure-theoretic isomorphism. We derive explicit formulas for finite probability-preserving systems and study finite periodic approximations of uniform Bernoulli shifts on symbols, proving that their system-level mean and variance invariants converge to and , respectively. Our results demonstrate that local periodic penetration and return-time statistics provide information about dynamical systems beyond that captured by classical entropy.

Original abstract

We investigate diffusion approximations for a class of multivariate inhomogeneous finite-velocity random motions motivated by models of active particles. A particle alternates cyclically among prescribed velocity fields with random run times and evolves according to either a flight dynamics, consisting of piecewise-linear motion, or a gliding dynamics, in which the particle follows the corresponding velocity flow. Original abstract

In this paper we study germs of holomorphic foliations in the complex plane that are Newton non-degenerate. This is an algebraic condition attached to the Newton polygon of the foliation, analogous to the one defined by Kouchnirenko for analytic curves and, more generally, hypersurfaces. As in this case, the condition of Newton non-degeneracy for foliations can be identified through the comparison of two numerical invariants: the Newton number - a computation involving simple geometric data of the Newton polygon - and the Milnor number. The Newton number is never greater than the Milnor number, and the foliation is Newton non-degenerate if and only if they are equal. The assumption that a foliation is Newton non-degenerate imposes conditions on its set of separatrices. We prove that, under this hypothesis, the foliation has no weak separatrices and, hence, all its separatrices are analytic. We also give a description of the set of separatrices of a Newton non-degenerate foliation, which is sufficient to characterize the foliation as Newton non-degenerate when it is of generalized curve type. This allows us to prove that the property of being Newton non-degenerate is topologically invariant for foliations of generalized curve type.
