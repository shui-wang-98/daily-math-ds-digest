Original abstract

We prove that a transitive monotone factor of a smooth surface diffeomorphism has a unique measure of maximal entropy whenever its topological entropy is positive. Its entropy is the entropy of a single homoclinic class of the diffeomorphism, and the factor map is a measure-theoretic isomorphism on every ergodic measure with positive entropy of that class. Original abstract

How often does an element of the geometric iterated Galois group of a Lattès map fix a vertex of the tree?
