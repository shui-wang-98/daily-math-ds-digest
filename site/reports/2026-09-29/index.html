We study forward orbits in generated by the expanding affine maps , where are integers and ranges over a nonempty digit set . We obtain explicit formulae for the mass, Beurling, discrete packing, and Assouad dimensions, the discrete Hausdorff dimension and its lower variant, and the lower entropy index. When , all these dimensions equal . When , they can differ, and several depend on the starting point through the sizes of the endpoint columns of . The two Hausdorff dimensions coincide and admit a pressure variational formula.
