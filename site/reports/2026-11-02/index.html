We introduce the notion of Boomerang subalgebras of group von Neumann algebras and study their general properties. We show that every Boomerang subalgebra of is the group von Neumann algebra of a Boomerang subgroup when is a countable -simple acylindrically hyperbolic group or a finite product of such groups. Original abstract

We introduce the median uniformity , an intrinsic precompact convex uniform structure on a median algebra. It is Hausdorff under natural assumptions, for instance for finite-rank median algebras. In the Hausdorff case, its uniform completion yields the Minimal Median Compactification (MMC). We study equivariant gradient degree comparisons for strongly indefinite Hamiltonian systems through Clarke duality. Under suitable convexity and compactness assumptions, we establish a correspondence between the primal and dual equations that preserves the symmetries of their solutions. We derive an identity relating their negative spectral representations and show that the Galerkin-normalized local index of the primal linearization coincides with the equivariant gradient degree determined by the dual Hessian. Original abstract

Equation-free forecast-error analysis is useful when governing equations, Jacobians, tangent dynamics, or full state vectors are unavailable and only scalar time series are observed. In this setting, the multi-horizon forecast-error curve can provide both an instability estimate and nonlinear predictive features, making its numerical reliability important. We show that long finite-precision chaotic records can enter an information-saturation regime in which increasing record length N (the total number of analyzed samples) adds repeated histories rather than new dynamical information. For the float32 logistic map, exact recurrence progressively fills the training catalogue with duplicate histories and identical futures, suppressing forecast-error growth and driving the estimated Lyapunov slope toward zero. Across 10,000 initial conditions, exact recurrence occurred by iteration 7612; across 28 stratified seeds, collapse onset strongly followed the recurrence-occupancy scale. A two-dimensional Hénon-map control confirms that recurrence and estimator failure are separate events: the float32 trajectory enters an exact machine-state cycle after a transient of samples, with cycle period P = 12,268, yet the forecast-error estimate remains close to the positive Jacobian/QR reference until catalogue saturation near N = 200,000. Casting an already generated float32 record to float64 does not restore lost information, whereas genuine float64 generation, restarted short trajectories, and one-ULP perturbations prevent collapse over the tested range. A recursive local-linear predictor exhibits the same endpoint at a later, predictor-dependent threshold. The results identify a recurrence-to-catalogue-saturation failure mode specific to forecast-error/analogue inference rather than Lyapunov estimation in general.
