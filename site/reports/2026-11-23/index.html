Almost every infinite-type interval translation map in the Bruin–Troubetzkoy family, and in Bruin's family with any fixed number of branches, is rigid for its invariant probability, where typicality is understood with respect to the intrinsic induction parameter measure. Original abstract

We study small scale quantum ergodicity in quantum cat maps, with semiclassical parameter 1/N, and prove three groups of results. First, for every N and every eigenbasis, almost all eigenstates are equidistributed on phase space balls of radius (log N)^(-a) for a<1/2, and on position space balls for a<1. For a set of N of full density and for Hecke eigenbases, almost all eigenstates, and even all of them, are equidistributed at polynomial scales. Second, every invariant probability measure is the semiclassical measure of quasimodes of order 1/log N, one for each N, that are not equidistributed at any scale between 4N^(-1/8) and a prescribed scale tending to zero. Third, there are orthonormal bases of quasimodes of order 1/log N for every N, and eigenbases for N with short periods, in which a full density subset is equidistributed at every fixed scale, but not at the scales (log N)^(-a) for a>1/2 in phase space and a>1 in position space. Hence, the exponents 1/2 and 1 in the first group are sharp: even for a full density subset of eigenstates, quantum ergodicity at fixed scales does not extend beyond the critical logarithmic scales.
