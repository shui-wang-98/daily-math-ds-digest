Original abstract

We establish a Hurewicz-type inequality relating mean dimension and conditional metric mean dimension. For every equivariant continuous map between dynamical systems, the mean dimension of the domain is bounded above by the sum of the mean dimension of the target and the conditional metric mean dimension of the map. In particular, any equivariant continuous map that decreases mean dimension must have infinite topological conditional entropy. The key ingredient is a new waist inequality, which we believe is of independent geometric interest beyond its application to dynamical systems. Compared with Gromov's original waist inequality, a distinctive feature of this inequality is that it applies to finite simplicial complexes of arbitrary topology, subject only to dimensional restrictions.

In the 1980s, Douady and Sullivan conjectured that the Siegel disks of rational maps with degree at least two are Jordan domains. Moreover, we prove that the Julia sets of these functions with a fixed bounded type Siegel disk are locally connected.
