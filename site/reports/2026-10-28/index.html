For closed negatively curved surfaces, we obtain formulas for the derivatives of the harmonic entropy of its geodesic flow and the linear drift of its Brownian motion along conformal deformations. Original abstract

We establish the variational characterization of the measure of maximal entropy on noncompact manifolds of nonpositive sectional curvature. Assuming that the deck transformation group contains an axial isometry with a rank-one axis and has a finite critical exponent, we prove that the supremum of the entropies of invariant probabilities equals the critical exponent and is attained if and only if the Bowen-Margulis-Sullivan (BMS) measure is finite. Original abstract

This paper shows that the wave trace of a bounded and strictly convex planar domain may be arbitrarily smooth in a neighborhood of some point in the length spectrum. In other words, the Poisson relation, which asserts that the singular support of the wave trace is contained in the closure of the length spectrum, can almost be made into a strict inclusion.
